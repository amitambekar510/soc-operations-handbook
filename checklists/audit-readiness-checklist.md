## 6 Weeks Before Audit

| # | Task | Owner | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm audit date, scope, and evidence requirements with auditor | SOC Manager | ☐ |
| 2 | Assign evidence coordinator | SOC Manager | ☐ |
| 3 | Refresh all standing evidence packages | Compliance Analyst | ☐ |
| 4 | Verify CERT-In reporting register is complete for audit period | Compliance Analyst | ☐ |

## 4 Weeks Before Audit

| # | Task | Owner | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Generate all SIEM report exports for audit period | Compliance Analyst | ☐ |
| 6 | Confirm all incidents have complete documentation | Compliance Analyst | ☐ |
| 7 | Verify platform availability metrics for audit period | Platform Engineer | ☐ |
| 8 | Confirm log source coverage matrix is current | Platform Engineer | ☐ |
| 9 | Verify all access reviews completed for audit period | Platform Engineer | ☐ |
| 10 | Confirm VAPT was conducted and findings are tracked | SOC Manager | ☐ |

## 2 Weeks Before Audit

| # | Task | Owner | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Deliver draft evidence package to client for review | CSM | ☐ |
| 12 | Identify and remediate any gaps found in dry run | SOC Manager | ☐ |
| 13 | Prepare auditor read-only access credentials | Platform Engineer | ☐ |
| 14 | Brief SOC team on audit scope and logistics | SOC Manager | ☐ |
